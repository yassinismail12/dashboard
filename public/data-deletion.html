Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data our AI customer-assistant service holds about you. Here is how.

If you are a customer who messaged or commented on a business

  1. Send a message on WhatsApp to +20 115 212 5932 with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us which business you contacted and the name or phone number you used, so we can find your records.
  3. We delete your conversations, comments, and any booking or lead details linked to you, and confirm to you when it is done. We act on requests within 30 days.

If you are a business that connected an account

  1. Remove the app from your Facebook account: Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites for Instagram) → select the app → Remove. This revokes our access immediately.
  2. Message us on WhatsApp at +20 115 212 5932 with the subject "Delete my account" to have your account, stored access tokens, knowledge data, conversations and bookings deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

Messages and replies, comment text, names and phone numbers given for bookings or enquiries, appointment records, and stored access tokens. Short-lived technical copies (14 days for comment diagnostics, 60 days for lead copies) expire automatically.
