Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data our AI customer-assistant service ("the Service") processes, why, and what rights people have. The Service is operated by Yassin Ismail ("we", "us"). It lets businesses ("Businesses") answer their own customers' questions automatically on Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and website chat, take bookings and enquiries, and reply to questions left in comments on their posts.

Who this applies to

  • Customers of a Business who message the Business's Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp number or website chat, or comment on its posts.
  • Businesses who sign up and connect their accounts to the Service.

What we process

  • Messages a customer sends to a Business, and the replies the assistant sends back.
  • Comments left on a connected Business's Facebook Page or Instagram posts (the comment text and the commenter's public name or ID), only to answer questions in them.
  • Contact and booking details a customer chooses to give: name, phone number, and the date and time of an appointment.
  • Business account data: the Business's name, email, the knowledge it provides (prices, hours, services, FAQs), and the access tokens that let the Service act on its connected Pages, Instagram and WhatsApp accounts.

How we use it

  • To answer the customer's question on behalf of the Business, in the language they wrote in.
  • To create, change or cancel a booking, and to tell the Business about new bookings, cancellations and enquiries (for example by WhatsApp).
  • To keep the Service working, secure, and accurate (for example, detecting errors).

Data obtained through Facebook and Instagram is used only to provide these replies for the Business that connected the account. We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles for any other purpose.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the Service, who process data on our behalf:

  • Meta Platforms (Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp), to send and receive messages.
  • OpenAI, to generate the text of replies from the message and the Business's knowledge.
  • MongoDB Atlas (database) and Render (hosting), to store data and run the Service.

The Business that a customer contacted can see that customer's conversation with its assistant.

How long we keep it

  • Conversations and bookings are kept while the Business's account is active, or until they are deleted on request.
  • Lead copies are kept for 60 days. Diagnostic copies of comment text are kept for 14 days.
  • When a Business closes its account, we delete its data on request within 30 days.

Your rights and deletion

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data. See the data deletion instructions. We act on requests within 30 days.

Security

Data is stored in access-controlled cloud services over encrypted connections, and access tokens are kept server-side. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access and review problems promptly.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
